# AI is just automation

[This talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yeH1DqK984) by Stuart Halloway makes a great point: AI is just automation; it carries the same disruptive potential, both positive and negative, as previous automations before it. 

He asks the questions that are valuable to software engineer beyond the reflex "does it work?" -- and give convincing answers: 
- What is AI? -> just a new form of automation. No existential risk, no conscience.
- Why is it (AI hype/using token output as a software quality metric) happening? -> because management thinks they can get away with it, the same way they got away with imposing such unrealistic objective to Amazon workers that they had to pee in bottles
- Who does it benefit? -> the ultra rich, with the help of lawmakers (cf. 2025 inauguration)

For all lazy (and therefore _good_) programmers, automating as much as possible with AI is enticing. But it comes with a significant loss of skill in autonomy (cf. the [study on schoolchildren's drop in tests results after allowing GenAI for homework](https://cepr.org/publications/dp21577)).
[Cognitive surrender](https://knlb.dev/essays/surrender#fnref2) has to be resisted actively: deciding when to take back control from coding agents and enforcing it, to gain the practical knowledge and experience that comes with friction.
